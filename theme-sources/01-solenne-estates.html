Our philosophy

Principles every
home is built on

Site first

Every design begins with the land: its light, slope, wind and views. We shape the house around the site, never the other way around, so each home feels as if it has always belonged there.

01

Honest materials

Stone, cedar, glass and raw concrete are left to age with dignity. We choose a few exceptional materials and detail them with care, rather than layering finishes for effect.

02

Quiet luxury

True comfort is felt, not announced. Silence, proportion, daylight and craftsmanship define our homes far more than ornament or excess ever could.

About us

Built slowly,
lived in for generations

Solenne Estates was founded in 2009 by a small group of architects and builders who believed a home should be a response to its place. Fifteen years on, we remain deliberately small, taking on a handful of residences each year so that every one receives our full attention.

From the first walk across the site to the final handover of keys, our in-house team of designers, engineers and craftspeople works as one. No intermediaries, no compromises, only homes we would be proud to live in ourselves.
