# 01 · Vision

## What we are building

The website of our web design and software agency. It is a **business profile that lets visitors act**:
learn, describe their project, and use working demos. It is not a template store.

## The core principle: sell value, not features

Business owners do not buy "a website with a booking form". They buy **more bookings, fewer phone calls,
more trust, more time**. Every page, section, and template answers: *what does this bring to the business?*

| Feature (what we avoid leading with) | Value (what we lead with) |
|---|---|
| Online booking form | Customers book at 2am without calling you |
| Admin dashboard | See every order and booking in one place, from your phone |
| Bilingual site | Reach Arabic- and English-speaking customers |
| Responsive design | Look professional on the phones your customers use |

## The problem we noticed

Many customers do not know the difference between a profile website, a functional website with a dashboard,
and a SaaS product. They ask for one thing and need another. So before choosing, they learn:
the **Software Guide** section explains the types and helps them pick.

## Why working demos

A static screenshot convinces nobody. Our demos are complete business websites with a working dashboard:
the visitor books a table on the site, opens the dashboard, and sees the booking. That moment sells.
Demo data is saved only in the visitor's browser, so there is no account and no setup.

## Who uses it

| Who | Goal |
|---|---|
| **Visitor** (business owner, mostly Middle East, Arabic or English) | Understand what they need and see it working |
| **Demo user** (the same visitor inside a template) | Experience the product as if it were theirs |
| **Admin** (the agency owner) | See visitors, leads, and which demos work, to improve and to follow up |

## Success looks like

- Visitors complete the wizard or click WhatsApp.
- Visitors register in demos and perform real actions there.
- The admin can see which fields and templates attract people and decide what to build next.

## Not in scope

- Real visitor accounts, payments, or selling templates online.
- Saving demo data on our server.
